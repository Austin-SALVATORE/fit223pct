**[Coach ruling — Inverted Row grip and illustration]**

Decision: **Option 1 — Keep the current one-hand-per-bar illustration.**

For the athlete's freestanding parallel-bar station, use a neutral-grip Inverted Row:

- Body positioned between the two parallel bars.
- One hand gripping each bar.
- Hands at approximately shoulder width.
- Pull the torso upward under control.
- Keep the trunk stable and avoid swinging.
- Adjust foot placement and body angle to control difficulty.

Update the coaching cue from "pull toward the bar" to **"pull your torso upward between the bars."**

No illustration redraw is required if the existing image accurately represents this setup.

**Safety requirement:** Before the first working set, verify that both independent bars remain stable under the intended pulling direction. If either bar moves, slides or tilts, do not perform this variation.

This is the approved movement configuration, subject to physical stability validation during the athlete's first session.
