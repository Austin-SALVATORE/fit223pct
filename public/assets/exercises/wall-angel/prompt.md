# Wall Angel — image generation prompt

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Exercise id | `wall-angel` |
| Category | Warm-up / Activation |
| Camera | `side` |
| Frames | 2 |
| Equipment | Wall — the whole back and both forearms/wrists rest against it; the wall is the guide surface for the movement, not balance support |
| Status | `planned` |

> Same wall treatment and true-profile camera as `wall-slide` (its nearest
> shipped analogue — coach classification, plan `wall-angel` §6.3). The
> difference from `wall-slide` is the range: the coach's cue is "use only a
> comfortable range of shoulder motion" — this movement stops short of the
> full overhead lockout `wall-slide` shows, and never reaches vertical.

## Prompt

```text
Create one wide instructional fitness illustration: a single horizontal strip
showing the SAME person performing one repetition, read left to right.

RENDERING STYLE (identical in every image):
Semi-realistic digital vector illustration. Smooth cel shading with soft
gradient blends. No visible outlines, no sketch linework, no cross-hatching, no
painterly brush texture, no halftone. Clean, premium, modern fitness-app
artwork. Even neutral studio lighting from the front-left. Soft form shading
only — no cast shadow on the ground, no dark occlusion pooling.

BACKGROUND:
Single flat solid chroma-key magenta #FF00FF, completely uniform and empty.
No floor, no ground line, no shadow cast onto the background, no gym
environment, no gradient, no vignette, no frame or border. The magenta is
ONLY the background: no magenta may appear anywhere in the artwork itself —
not in skin, hair, clothing, or equipment. No drop shadow, no glow, and no
halo of any kind may surround the figure or equipment — artwork edges end
crisply at the artwork, with pure background touching them directly.

CHARACTER (must be the same woman in every image and every frame):
One adult woman, athletic and lean, visible but not exaggerated muscle
definition, mid-to-late twenties. Warm medium-tan skin: highlight #FAC497,
midtone #EBA878, shadow #CE8254. Dark near-black brown hair, #2B201D with
#3F2F28 highlights, pulled into a high ponytail that hangs behind the shoulder
and follows the movement naturally. Softly defined realistic face, subtle
natural makeup, calm and confident neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open
and looking in the direction the movement faces.

WARDROBE (identical in every image):
Steel denim-blue racerback sports bra, #2C4F6C, midriff exposed.
Deep navy-charcoal high-waisted full-length leggings, #31384A, highlights
#353C4E, shadows #1D222F.
Clean white low-profile sneakers with white soles, #FEFEFE, shading #E9E9EA.
No visible socks, no jewelry, no watch, no logos, no text or graphics on any
clothing.

EQUIPMENT RENDERING:
Matte dark charcoal metal #1D2025 with brushed chrome shafts and handles
#EFEFEF. Weight plates are mid-value charcoal gray #4A5058, never pure black.
Upholstery and bench pads matte near-black #1D2025. Every dark surface
carries a subtle cool rim light along its upper and outer edges so equipment
stays legible against dark app backgrounds. Simple, clean, realistic
proportions with believable weight and correct scale against the body.

COMPOSITION:
All figures stand on one shared invisible ground line, at exactly the same
scale. Each pose occupies its own exclusive vertical band running the full
height of the image: a wide column of solid background magenta separates it
from every neighboring pose on both sides, and no part of any pose —
including hands, feet, or equipment — may share a horizontal (left-right)
position with any part of another pose, even when the two sit at different
heights. A straight vertical line drawn anywhere in a gap must be able to
pass from the top of the image to the bottom without touching either
neighboring pose. No figure overlaps, touches, or is cropped by another. The
entire body is visible in every frame, including both feet. Generous
background margin above and below. Eye-level camera at an identical angle and
distance for every frame.

STRICTLY EXCLUDE:
Text, numbers, labels, captions, arrows, motion lines, panel borders, dividing
lines, grids, watermarks, logos, background objects, mirrors, other people,
extra or missing limbs, distorted hands, any magenta on the figure, clothing or equipment, drop shadows or glow halos around the figure, broken or hyperextended joints.

MOVEMENT FOR THIS IMAGE:
Exercise: Wall Angel
Camera: TRUE side profile view — she is seen in a strict, flat profile,
facing to the RIGHT of the frame, with her whole back against a wall
positioned at the frame's LEFT edge. This is a true profile, not a
three-quarter angle: only one eye and one side of the nose bridge are
visible, the far shoulder is hidden directly behind the near shoulder, and
the far arm is hidden behind the torso and is NOT visible in either frame.
Only the NEAR arm is visible in both frames — do not draw a second arm or
any part of one; the camera angle must not drift toward front-facing, which
is what would expose it. This is the same true-profile camera `wall-slide`
uses, and it is deliberate: any drift toward three-quarter reads as a
pose defect, not a stylistic variant.
Equipment: a wall. Render it as a single flat, FILLED vertical plane, matte
warm charcoal #4A4138 — the app's own mid-value border register, shifted
warm. It must be unmistakably, clearly visible against the background — not
near-invisible — while staying clearly darker and less saturated than her
skin, hair and white sneakers, so she stays the brightest thing in the
frame. No gradient, no rim light, no texture, no floor line, no baseboard,
no corner.
The wall must NEVER float in open space with background visible on both
sides of it — that reads as a free-standing column, not a wall, and is
wrong. It is CUT OFF by the picture's own edge, on the LEFT in both frames,
directly behind her. In frame 1: the wall's flat color runs off the true
left edge of the entire generated strip — zero magenta is visible between
the wall and that outer border, as if the wall simply continues past what
the picture shows. Unlike every other piece of equipment in this library,
the wall gets no padding or background margin on that side. Between frame 1
and frame 2, leave only the minimum clean strip of plain magenta needed to
tell the two frames apart — noticeably narrower than the wide, generous
spacing used elsewhere in this library. In frame 2: the wall's flat color
sits hard against that narrow gap, not set back from it, so there is
essentially no open background visible on the wall's left side there
either. The wall runs the full height of the frame, top to bottom, with no
gap above or below, in both frames.
CRITICAL, different from every other wall in this library, and true in BOTH
frames without exception: her body is NOT standing apart from the wall with
background between them. Her upper back and the back of her head are in
DIRECT, CONTINUOUS PHYSICAL CONTACT with the wall's flat surface — there is
ZERO gap, and absolutely no strip of magenta background, between the wall's
right-hand edge and her back. The wall's right-hand edge and the silhouette
of her upper back and head are the SAME LINE in the image: her body begins
exactly where the wall's flat color ends, touching it directly. She does
not float free in open background near the wall — she is pressed against
it. This applies identically in frame 1 and frame 2: raising the arm higher
in frame 2 does NOT pull her body away from the wall even slightly — if
there is any visible magenta strip between her back and the wall in either
frame, that is wrong.
Number of frames: 2, evenly spaced left to right.
Both figures stand on the same shared invisible ground line as each other, at
exactly identical scale and spacing, each with her back against her own copy
of the wall.

Frames:
1. Goalpost start. Standing tall with her whole upper back, shoulder blades
   and the back of her head PRESSED DIRECTLY AGAINST the wall — her
   silhouette touches the wall's flat color with zero gap, no strip of
   magenta visible between her back and the wall anywhere along its height.
   Feet planted a small step out from the wall so her weight stacks
   correctly while the back stays flush, knees soft. The NEAR arm only is
   raised into a goalpost/W shape: upper arm out at shoulder height, elbow
   bent to roughly 90 degrees, forearm pointing straight up, with the back
   of the forearm and wrist resting flat against the wall's surface — a
   visible contact line along the forearm, not just the fingertips. Ribs
   pulled down, lower back gently flattened toward the wall without forcing
   it, chin level. The far arm is hidden behind the torso and is not drawn.
2. Partial slide, comfortable range — DELIBERATELY SMALL, NOT overhead. The
   same standing position against the same wall: her whole upper back,
   shoulder blades and the back of her head are STILL PRESSED DIRECTLY
   AGAINST the wall — her silhouette still touches the wall's flat color
   with zero gap, no strip of magenta visible between her back and the wall
   anywhere along its height, exactly as in frame 1; her body has not moved
   away from the wall at all. The NEAR arm has slid only MODESTLY higher than
   frame 1's goalpost position: the elbow opens from frame 1's ~90 degrees
   to at most 120 degrees — a clearly visible bend remains at the elbow,
   roughly a quarter to a third of the way from frame 1's bent goalpost
   toward a full overhead reach, and NO FURTHER. The arm is NOT straight.
   The wrist rises only to about EYEBROW-TO-EYE height — it must NOT pass
   the top of the head, and it is nowhere close to fully extended straight
   above the head. If the arm looks close to straight, or the hand is
   clearly above the top of the head, that is WRONG — redraw it lower and
   more bent. The forearm and wrist still maintain light contact with the
   wall's surface. Shoulders stay down, away from the ears — they do not
   hike up to chase a bigger range. Ribs still pulled down, lower back
   still gently flattened toward the wall, feet unmoved. The far arm
   remains hidden behind the torso and is not drawn.

TECHNIQUE — must be correct in every frame:
- TRUE profile camera in both frames: only one eye/one side of the nose
  bridge visible, the far shoulder and far arm are anatomically hidden
  behind the torso and must not be drawn or implied in either frame.
- Her back is in DIRECT CONTACT with the wall — zero gap, no visible strip
  of magenta background between her silhouette and the wall's flat color,
  in both frames. This is the single most important thing to get right.
- The upper back, shoulder blades and head stay lightly touching the wall in
  both frames — she does not lean forward away from it.
- The back of the near forearm and wrist stays in light contact with the
  wall's flat surface throughout, from the bent goalpost shape in frame 1
  to the partial slide in frame 2 — a continuous sliding contact, not a
  lift-and-place.
- Frame 1 and frame 2 must read as a clearly different pair at a glance:
  frame 1 is a bent goalpost/W with the elbow near shoulder height; frame 2
  is a slightly more open angle with the elbow a little higher — but the
  elbow opens to AT MOST 120 degrees (from frame 1's ~90) and the wrist
  rises to AT MOST eyebrow-to-eye height. The arm must NOT look straight or
  close to straight, and the hand must NOT be above the top of the head.
  This is a small, conservative movement — roughly a quarter to a third of
  the way toward a full overhead reach, and no further. It is a smaller,
  comfortable range, not the full overhead slide shown in the wall-slide
  exercise; if frame 2 looks like a full overhead reach, it is wrong.
- Shoulders stay down, away from the ears, in both frames — they do not
  shrug upward to gain height.
- Ribs stay pulled down and the lower back stays gently flat against the
  wall in both frames — no arching away from it to force the reach.
- Feet stay planted, a small step out from the wall, in both frames.
- The wall renders as a single flat, filled vertical plane in matte warm
  charcoal #4A4138 — no texture, no floor line, no corner, no gradient, no
  rim light — cropped at the frame's own edge, flush with no visible gap.
```
